## Design Context

### Users
Networking students in college/university courses (e.g., CIS/IT programs) who need to drill IP subnetting for coursework, labs, and eventually certifications. They use this in focused study sessions — likely before an exam or as assigned practice. They may feel mild anxiety about subnetting math and need an environment that builds confidence through repetition.

### Brand Personality
**Three words**: Focused, precise, confident

The interface should feel like a well-designed study tool — purposeful, not flashy. It should reduce cognitive load and put the question front and center. Think: a polished practice exam, not an arcade game.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light-first with a capable dark mode. Not the generic Tailwind blue-gray default.
- **Visual tone**: Clean with strong typographic hierarchy. Dense where it needs to be, generous where it doesn't.
- **Anti-references**: No blue-gray card grids. No glowing neon on dark. No gradient text headings. No glassmorphism. No "AI slop" color palette (cyan-on-dark, purple-to-blue gradients).
- **References**: Think Anki's focused simplicity, or a well-designed textbook exercise. The networking domain should subtly inform the aesthetic — structure, hierarchy, precision — without going "terminal cosplay."
- **Color**: Avoid pure Tailwind defaults. Consider a warm or slightly tinted neutral base. The accent color should feel intentional, not "blue because blue is safe."

### Design Principles
1. **Question first**: The question text is the hero. Everything else is secondary or tertiary.
2. **No noise**: Decorative elements must earn their place. If it doesn't aid comprehension or orientation, remove it.
3. **Feedback is teaching**: Correct/incorrect states should feel informative, not punishing. The explanation card is a feature, not an afterthought.
4. **Confidence through clarity**: Typography, spacing, and color should reduce anxiety, not add to it.
5. **Distinctive without being distracting**: The design should be memorable for being well-crafted, not for being visually loud.
